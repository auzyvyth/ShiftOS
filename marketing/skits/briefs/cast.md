# Drevo Skits: cast brief (first draft, 2026-11-22)

Paste this with `preview/cast-sheet-desktop.png` into ChatGPT or Gemini. Bring their versions back as
pictures + notes; the drawings are rebuilt in `tools/rig.mjs`, never pasted in as AI images.

## What the skits are
Short Malay comedy cartoons (TikTok / Reels, 9:16) about buying a used car in Malaysia. An honest Drevo
salesman shows a first-time buyer the tricks other sellers pull (flood cars, wiped engines, hidden costs).
Funny first, useful second.

## Style (keep)
- Flat 2D cartoon, thick dark ink outline (#16181d), flat colour plus ONE shadow tone, no gradients.
- Big round head on a short body. Same body for everyone; only skin, hair and clothes change.
- Faces are swappable parts: about 15 eye shapes and 10 mouths, so expressions stay simple and bold.
- Drevo red #dc2626 belongs to the Drevo salesman. Nobody else wears it as their main colour.

## Rules (do not break)
- **Nobody's race is the joke.** The cast is a Malaysian mix on purpose. Characters differ by job,
  clothes and personality, never by an accent, a head movement or any gesture tied to an ethnicity.
- The person in the Drevo shirt is never the con man.
- No real car brand logos, dealer names or real people.
- Never flip a character left-right (the shirt logo would read backwards).

## What to ask them for
1. Make each character more distinct and memorable from the silhouette alone (shape of hair, outfit).
2. Stronger, funnier expressions that still use the same simple eye + mouth parts.
3. Keep the style rules above; suggest at most one change to the body shape, with a reason.
4. Flag anything that could read as a stereotype or offend a Malaysian viewer.
