## 2. Acting direction
- **Faiz**: 1 excited, sure it's a bargain. 6 real disgust and confusion. 9 dramatic shock, stress "LUMPUR". 13 follows Mak at once.
- **Bos Zack**: 2 extremely confident, big smile + thumbs up. 5 getting uncomfortable. 11 stutters, looks away, forced smile. 13 desperate.
- **Mak Som**: 3 slow suspicious squint, silent. 12 emotionless, "Faiz. Kita balik." slow and firm. 13 walks off without looking back.
- **Abang Jo**: 4 calm, experienced. 7-10 confident, no exaggeration. 14 to camera, helpful, not salesy.

## 5. Real photos (original, of the staged scenes; never maker or stock photos)
1. Red Myvi, side profile, against a PLAIN background (it is cut out and placed on Bos Zack's drawn lot).
2. Interior through the open driver's door, seats + floor. 3. Carpet pulled back, soil visible. 4. Headlamp with
condensation inside. Same car throughout, consistent light, no dealership branding, no readable number plate.

## 6. Editing notes
Quick cuts between lines and reactions. Comedic pause after Mak's stare. Suspense sting when the carpet lifts,
a dramatic hit on "LUMPUR". Pause before "embun pagi". Comedic silence after "Kita balik". Big captions for phones.

## 7. Takeaway
1. Musty smell. 2. Mud under the carpets. 3. Condensation inside the headlamps. Warning signs, not proof:
a proper inspection is still recommended. Final message: "Jangan mudah percaya bila seller kata kereta tak
pernah masuk air. Check dulu sebelum beli." This is education; Drevo does not inspect or certify cars.
