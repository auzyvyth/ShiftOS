# ShiftOS — 60-Second Loom Script

Target: under 60 seconds. ~150 words spoken. Record at 1280-wide, notifications off.
Have two tabs ready: salesman panel + dealer dashboard (Stock P&L modal pre-opened).

---

## 0:00 — Hook (8s)
**Screen:** XDrive storefront / a clean listing.
**Say:**
> "If you sell used cars in Malaysia, your stock, leads and commissions are scattered across six WhatsApp groups. ShiftOS puts it all in one place."

## 0:08 — Salesmen (17s)
**Screen:** Salesman panel → add a car to My Deals → open storefront link → move a lead to "won".
**Say:**
> "Salesmen get their own storefront — feature any dealer car instantly, share your link, and every lead comes straight to you. Your commission shows before you sell, and the second you close, the handover checklist builds itself. Start free."

## 0:25 — Dealers (26s)
**Screen:** Dealer dashboard → Overview → open Stock P&L modal → Handover checklist → Customers expiry.
**Say:**
> "Dealers see real profit per car — sale price minus purchase, recon, commission and handover costs, so you know what each unit actually made. Run your whole team and pipeline on one board. Every win auto-builds the Puspakom and pindah-milik checklist, and ShiftOS reminds you before each customer's road tax expires — so you resell to them."

## 0:51 — Close (8s)
**Screen:** Back to storefront / logo.
**Say:**
> "Stock, leads, profit and follow-up in one system. Salesmen free, dealers from RM299. Hit the link and I'll set you up today."
