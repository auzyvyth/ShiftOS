# ShiftOS — 60-Second Loom Script (Production Cut)

Voiceover stays under 60s (~150 words). Everything else here is direction:
exact screens, real button labels, captions, and cursor moves so the video
(or a design pass) can be built shot-for-shot.

---

## Production setup
- **Resolution:** record 1920x1080, UI zoomed so text is readable on mobile playback.
- **Accounts ready:** a demo dealer with 6+ listings, 3+ leads in CRM, ONE won deal that has F&I add-ons + a handover in progress, and one salesman on the Team.
- **Tabs pre-opened:** (1) salesman panel, (2) dealer dashboard on Inventory, (3) the Stock P&L modal already loaded for the IS300 so it opens instantly.
- **Cursor:** use a click-highlight (yellow ring). Move slow, pause 0.5s on each button before clicking.
- Notifications off. One continuous take per part; cut between parts.

## On-screen caption + button style (match the brand)
- **Lower-third caption pill:** dark #080C14 bg, white text, 1px #1f2937 border, 10px radius, 16px padding. Use for the spoken headline of each scene.
- **CTA button (end card):** red #dc2626 bg, white text, 12px radius, bold. Label: "Start free →".
- **Highlight accent (arrows / underlines):** red #dc2626.
- Dealer dashboard is LIGHT (white cards, #111827 text); marketplace is DARK (#080C14). Don't recolor the real UI — only overlays use these tokens.

---

## 0:00-0:08 — Hook (8s)
- **Screen:** public marketplace homepage, slow scroll over live car cards.
- **Caption:** "Stock, leads, commissions — all in six WhatsApp groups?"
- **Voiceover:**
  > "If you sell used cars in Malaysia, your stock, leads and commissions are scattered everywhere. ShiftOS puts it all in one place."

## 0:08-0:25 — Salesmen (17s)
- **Screen:** salesman panel.
  1. Click **Listings** tab → click **Available Inventory** sub-tab → on a car, click **"Add to my listings"** (the red Plus button). [3s]
  2. Click **Share Listing** (ShareMenu) → cut to the salesman's public storefront with their name/WhatsApp. [4s]
  3. Click **Leads** tab → drag/advance one lead to **Deposit to reserve**, then to **won**. [4s]
  4. Cut to **Handover** tab → the checklist auto-appears for that won deal. [3s]
- **Caption (sequence):** "Your storefront." → "Your leads." → "Commission auto-tracked." → "Handover builds itself."
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Salesmen get their own storefront — feature any dealer car instantly and share your link. Every lead lands with you, your commission shows before you sell, and the moment you close, the handover checklist builds itself. Start free."

## 0:25-0:51 — Dealers (26s)
- **Screen:** dealer dashboard (light theme).
  1. **Overview** tab — pause on units sold + GP this month. [3s]
  2. **Inventory** tab → open the IS300 → click the green **"View P&L"** button. Zoom the modal: Purchase, Recon, Commission, Holding, then the **"Sold with"** add-ons block and **Back gross**, landing on **Net P&L**. [9s — this is the money shot, hold it]
  3. **Leads / CRM** tab — drag a lead across the pipeline. [4s]
  4. **Handover** tab → open the 8-step checklist (Puspakom B5/B7, JPJ pindah milik, road tax, geran). [5s]
  5. **Customers** tab → cursor rests on a road-tax / insurance expiry date. [3s]
- **Caption (sequence):** "Real profit per car." → "Add-ons counted too." → "Whole team, one board." → "Handover, done right." → "Reminders = repeat sales."
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Dealers see real profit per car — sale price minus purchase, recon, commission, and even the add-ons it sold with, so you know what each unit actually made. Run your whole team on one board. Every win auto-builds the Puspakom and pindah-milik checklist, and ShiftOS reminds you before each customer's road tax expires — so you resell to them."

## 0:51-0:60 — Close (9s)
- **Screen:** snap back to the marketplace / ShiftOS logo. Overlay the red **"Start free →"** CTA button + pricing line.
- **Caption:** "Salesmen free · Dealers from RM299/mo"
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Stock, leads, profit and follow-up — one system. Salesmen free, dealers from RM299. Hit the link and I'll set you up today."
